# EDA Takeaways

- Humidity and mushroom yield appear positively related.
- Temperature remains within a narrow range, suggesting stable growing conditions.
- CO₂ levels vary more than temperature and humidity.
- Yield shows relatively low variation across the year.

##Conclusion
The growing environment appears stable throughout the year. Humidity may have a positive relationship with yield,while temperature remains consistent. Further modeling can be used to quantify the impact of environmental variables on mushroom productions.
